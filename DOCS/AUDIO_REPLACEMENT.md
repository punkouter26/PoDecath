# Replacing the synthesised audio

Every sound in the game is generated by `PoDecath/Bake Audio Clips` (`AudioBakery.cs`) into
`Assets/Audio/`, and wired by name into `Assets/Audio/AudioBank.asset`. It is a complete, reproducible
placeholder set: the mix, the crowd ring, the ducking, the occlusion and the Doppler all work on it and
will work unchanged on real recordings. Nothing else in the game references a clip by file; **everything
goes through the bank**, so a replacement is one drag onto one slot.

This page is what each slot wants, and which to do first.

## Do these first

Ranked by how much of the race they cover. The crowd bed is under every second of every event; the
sting is heard for half a second at a time.

| # | Slot | What it wants | Why it is first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `crowdBed` | 30–60 s **seamless loop**, stadium ambience with no distinct cheers or voices in it. The mix rides its *volume*, not its content, so a loop with a cheer in it will cheer at the wrong moment for ever. | under the whole event |
| 2 | `footfalls` (4) | four different single footfalls on asphalt, 150–300 ms, hard transient, dry. Picked round-robin per step; sixteen athletes make hundreds a minute, so any two that sound alike become a rhythm. | the second most-heard sound |
| 3 | `crowdSwell` | 3–5 s rising cheer, peak near the end, natural tail. Played on a finisher, a landing, the end of the event. | every result |
| 4 | `crowdGroan` | 2–3 s. The other noise: somebody has gone down. | every fall |
| 5 | `windBed` | 30 s+ **seamless loop**, wind with no gusts. It is louder the higher the camera goes; a gust would read as a camera move. | under every high shot |
| 6 | `pistol` | one starting pistol, 300–600 ms, with the echo the roof would give it | every start |
| 7 | `breath` | one breath cycle (in and out), 1.5–2.5 s, **seamless loop**. Pitched and slowed per athlete as it tires, so record it calm; the effort meter adds the strain. | one per athlete, all race |

## Then these

| Slot | What it wants |
|---|---|
| `footfallsSand` (3), `footfallsRubber` (3) | as `footfalls`, on sand (soft, grainy, almost no slap) and on the poured runway (duller and lower than asphalt) |
| `crowdApplause` | 4–8 s of settled, rhythmic applause — the noise *after* something is decided, not the roar during it |
| `crowdChant` | 8–16 s **seamless loop** of a rhythmic clap. Faded in when a race is close, out when it is not |
| `countBeep` | one short beep, 100–150 ms; the countdown plays it three times and pitches the last |
| `lapBell` | one bell, 1–2 s with tail; the leader starting the last lap |
| `hurdleClatter` | a hurdle going clean over: the bar, the frame, the deck, 1–1.5 s |
| `hurdleClip` | a hurdle clipped but left standing: shorter, brighter, and far more common than the clatter |
| `sandThud` | a body landing in a pit, 300–500 ms |
| `whoosh` | 300–500 ms, under a camera cut, well down in the mix |
| `sting` | a 0.5–1 s musical stab under a lower third or a result |
| `musicCalm`, `musicDrive`, `musicPeak` | three **stems of one piece**: same tempo, same key, and **exactly the same length** (whole bars, seamless loops). `MusicDirector` starts all three on one DSP sample and only moves their volumes — calm alone before the race, drive added at the gun, peak added when the race is tight or in its last stretch. Stereo is fine. The importer takes files named `calm` / `drive` / `peak` under a `music/` folder and keeps them sample for sample (no trim, no normalising) |

## Format

- **WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit.** Mono for anything that is placed in the world (footfalls, hurdle hits,
  the sand, breath): the spatialiser makes it stereo. Stereo is fine for the beds and the sting.
- **Peak around −3 dBFS, no limiting.** The mix buses set the levels (`AudioMix.cs`); a clip that is
  already loud has nowhere to go when a swell is ducked under the commentary.
- **Loops must be seamless** at the sample: no fade at either end, and check the join by playing it
  looped in any editor before importing. The four loops (`crowdBed`, `windBed`, `crowdChant`, `breath`)
  are the ones that will be noticed if they click.
- **Footfalls are transients**: trim the silence before the hit to nothing. A footfall that starts
  20 ms late is a footfall that lands after the foot does.

## How to swap one in

1. Drop the file anywhere under `Assets/Audio/` (a `Real/` folder keeps them apart from the baked set).
2. Select `Assets/Audio/AudioBank.asset`, drag the clip onto its slot. For the footfall arrays, set the
   size and fill the elements; any count works, the bank picks round-robin over whatever is there.
3. Press play. There is nothing to rebuild: every emitter reads the bank at the moment it plays.

`PoDecath/Bake Audio Clips` rewrites the *generated* files, not the bank's references, so re-baking
does not undo a replacement. Deleting a generated file that the bank still points at leaves that slot
silent, with a warning naming it.

## Importing a whole pack in one go (2026-09-29)

`PoDecath/Audio/Import Sound Pack...` fills the bank from a folder of WAV files. It was written for the
**Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundle** (free, royalty-free, commercial use allowed, no attribution required;
download the zips from sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc and unzip them anywhere), and works on any folder whose
files have descriptive names.

1. Run the menu item, pick the folder, choose **Dry run** first. It writes
   `training/logs/soundpack_import.json`: which file it would put in which slot, and which slots found
   nothing.
2. Run it again and choose **Import**. Each pick is converted to what this page asks for (mono for
   placed sounds, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, leading silence trimmed, cut to length with a fade, the four loops
   cross-faded into themselves, peak at -3 dBFS), written to `Assets/Audio/Real/`, and wired onto the
   bank. `Assets/Audio/Real/SOURCES.txt` records where every file came from.
3. Listen, and drag a different clip onto any slot you do not like. The baked set is untouched.

From the command line: write `{"folder": "D:/Sonniss", "dryRun": true}` to
`training/logs/soundpack.json` and run `unity command menu --path "PoDecath/Audio/Import Sound Pack (config)"`.
Tested on 2026-09-29 against a generated pack (48 kHz 24-bit stereo, 250-400 ms of silence before
each hit, decoys such as a pistol reload and a door bell): 14 slots filled, both decoys rejected,
silence trimmed to 2 ms, peaks at -3.0 dBFS. It has not yet been run on the real bundle.

## Where to get them

The project has no sample library and none is bundled. Freesound (CC0 filter) and the BBC Sound
Effects archive (RemArc licence, personal and research use only — check before shipping) cover every
slot above; a stadium bed and a starting pistol are among the most-recorded sounds there are. Record
the footfalls: fifteen minutes with a phone on a car park gives four asphalt footfalls that are yours.
