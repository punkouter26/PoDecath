The city around the grounds

2026-09-29 · made in Blender, exported to Assets/Models/Surroundings.glb, in all four rooftop scenes

What changed: the ring of 200 flat grey boxes that stood in for Washington became buildings: limestone, red brick and glass towers with windows and floor lines, flat roofs, and plant rooms on 116 of them. The obelisk is marble with the real monument's change of stone about a quarter of the way up. A ring of streets and blocks now runs from 410 m out, with grass down the Mall, so the blocks past the edge of the lawn no longer float over the sky.

Cost: 2,700 more triangles (5,372 in all) and a 1.5 MB model, drawn without shadows, probes or baked light, as the old boxes were. The track, the building and everything athletes touch are unchanged.

BEFORE (as found)NEW SCENE, LIGHTMAPS OFFNEW SCENE, BAKED

The race scene had been rebuilt earlier today without a lighting bake, so it was drawn without baked light. Rebuilding for the city and baking as the project's recipe says brings the baked light back, and the baked light makes the lawn a lighter, flatter green. Switching the lightmaps off gives the old deep green again (middle), so the city is not the cause. If the deeper green is the look you want, the bake's indirect light on the lawn is the setting to lower; I have not changed it.

The export checklist, applied

StepWhat was done
Metric, scale 1.0, pivotsMetres at 1.0. Transforms applied into the meshes; every part's origin is the city centre on the ground, the one point Unity's builder knows.
Topology and normalsDuplicate vertices merged, triangle pairs rejoined as quads, faces turned outward, flat shading. The export stops if any face points down or any exported normal disagrees with its face (both happened on the first try and made the ground invisible in Unity).
Procedural to PBRSeven materials built as procedural nodes (kept in the .blend as *_Procedural to re-bake), baked to Base Colour (sRGB), a packed ORM (R occlusion, G roughness, B metallic) and a tangent-space normal map. Unity reads one ORM texture for all three.
UVs and lightmapsUV0 tiles in metres; UV1 is a non-overlapping layout with 4-texel padding at 1024. Unity is told not to lightmap the ring (a 2 km ring would take most of the atlas for scenery in the fog), so UV1 is there if that changes.
Clean exportOwn collection under one root, orphan data purged, single-sided materials, glb with tangents, +Y up.
