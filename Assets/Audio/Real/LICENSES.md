## Notes

- Nothing here was auditioned by ear: segments were chosen from descriptions plus loudness/onset analysis. Listen before shipping, especially `crowdGroan`, `crowdBed` (check for PA/voices) and the music layers.
- `countBeep` is a recording of the BBC 1 kHz time-signal pip — a pure tone, so it will sound much like the synthesised placeholder.
- `footfallsRubber` are sneaker steps on concrete, low-passed at 3.5 kHz; no CC0 recording on a poured rubber track was found.
- `crowdChant` is built from a small group clapping in unison, re-timed to exactly 120 claps/min and doubled; it is not a large stadium crowd.
- Music: NotMeat2020's 'SANTUARIO' stem pack (150 BPM, F minor, trap/electronic). calm = atmosphere arps + soft lead; drive = + bass synth + half-level percussion; peak = all four stems. Same tempo, key and bar grid, so they can be cross-faded at any point. The author asks (not requires) to be credited as 'NotMeat2020 Beats'.
